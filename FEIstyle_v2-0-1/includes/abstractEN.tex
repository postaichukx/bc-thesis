This bachelor thesis deals with the analysis of blockchain transactions
using graph models and machine learning methods. The aim of the thesis is
to review existing approaches to blockchain data analysis, focus on the
detection of suspicious transactions related to money laundering, and
design an experimental model. The thesis investigates the representation
of blockchain transactions as graphs, the use of graph neural networks,
and risk-scoring methods. The proposed approach will be experimentally
evaluated on selected blockchain data and assessed in terms of its ability
to detect anomalous or potentially illicit transactions.
